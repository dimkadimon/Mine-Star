MINE STAR

Minesweeper among the stars — now with normal mines (1pt) and heavy mines (2pts). Numbers show total points nearby.

Choose your sector
📖 How to play (30 seconds)
  1. Scan sectors — click / single-tap (or Space) to reveal. First scan is always safe.
  2. Read the numbers — a number = sum of mine points touching that cell. A 💜 heavy mine counts as 2.
  3. Flag both types — Right-click / double-tap cycles 🚩 normal (1pt) → 💜 heavy (2pts) → clear (long-press works too). Keys 1/2 place directly, F cycles.
  4. Chord — click an uncovered number whose surrounding flag-points match to blast open the rest (C).
  5. Win — scan every safe sector and flag every mine with the correct type. Fastest time tops the leaderboard!

💡 On touch: single-tap scans, double-tap flags 🚩→💜 — or use the ⛏️/🚩/💜 mode buttons. Stuck? Press H for a hint (+5s).
